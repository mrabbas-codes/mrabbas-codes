### 🧑‍🚀 About Me

I'm a **frontend developer** based in **Mashhad, Iran** 🌍, passionate about crafting **intuitive, high-performance interfaces**. After a career switch 3 years ago, I found my true calling in frontend – from HTML to modern frameworks. I love turning complex requirements into smooth, delightful user experiences.

- 🔭 Currently deepening my expertise in **React**, **Next.js**, and **TypeScript**.
- 🌱 Learning advanced **Tailwind** patterns and **Framer Motion** for buttery animations.
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on **cutting-edge frontend projects** (SaaS, dashboards, design systems).
- 🎨 Bonus: I also work with **Photoshop/Illustrator** to bridge design & code.
- ⚡ 2025 goal: Release a **Next.js starter kit** and contribute to OSS.
